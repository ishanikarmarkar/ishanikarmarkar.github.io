We study the problem of computing an $\epsilon$-approximate saddle point of $\ell_2$-$\ell_1$ (perceptron) and $\ell_1$-$\ell_1$ (zero-sum) matrix games given access to only a small number of matrix-vector products. We provide an algorithm that provably converges to an $\epsilon$-approximate Nash equilibrium in $\tilde{O}(\epsilon^{-2/3})$ matrix-vector products. This improves upon our prior work, and our results match the (recently improved) lower bounds of Kornowski and Shamir (up to polylogarithmic factors). (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02347">arxiv</a>)
